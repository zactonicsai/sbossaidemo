# Sweet Crumb Bakery — Frequently Asked Questions

## Hours & Location

We're located at 412 Maple Street, Burlington. Open Tuesday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We're closed Mondays for prep and a much-needed day of rest.

For Sundays, the bakery closes at 3:00 PM (we keep early hours so our team can be home for family time).

We're closed for major holidays: Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day, and New Year's Day. We also close at noon on Christmas Eve and New Year's Eve.

## Custom Cake Orders

We need at least 72 hours' notice for custom cakes. For wedding cakes, we ask for 4 weeks' notice. Larger sheet cakes (serving 30+) require 1 week.

To order a custom cake, please call the bakery at (802) 555-0142 or email orders@sweetcrumb.example. We do not take custom cake orders through Instagram DMs because messages get lost there.

For all custom cakes, a 50% deposit is required at the time of ordering. The balance is due at pickup.

## Allergens & Dietary Options

All of our products are made in a kitchen that handles wheat, eggs, dairy, and tree nuts. We cannot guarantee any item is free from cross-contamination.

We offer the following dedicated allergen-aware products:
- **Gluten-free**: We have GF chocolate cake, GF chocolate chip cookies, and a rotating GF muffin daily.
- **Vegan**: Vegan chocolate cake, vegan banana bread, and most of our scones can be made vegan with 24 hours' notice.
- **Nut-free**: Most of our breads are nut-free, but please ask staff to confirm for any specific item.

We do not currently offer keto, paleo, or sugar-free products.

## Wedding Cakes

We offer wedding cakes starting at $8 per slice for buttercream designs and $10 per slice for fondant work. Most weddings need 1 slice per guest plus 10% extra. Tastings are by appointment only and cost $40 for up to 4 people, refundable against your order.

## Pre-orders & Pickup

Loaves of bread, dozens of pastries, and platters can be pre-ordered for next-day pickup if ordered before 2:00 PM. Same-day requests are usually possible but not guaranteed during weekends.

Pickup window is during regular hours. We don't hold orders past closing time. If you can't pick up, please call us — we'd rather give it to a friend than waste it.

## Delivery

We deliver within 5 miles for orders over $100, with a $25 delivery fee. We use a third-party courier and request 24 hours' notice. Same-day delivery is sometimes possible — call to ask.

## Payment

We accept cash, all major credit cards, and Apple Pay. We do not accept checks except for wedding orders over $500. There's a $0.50 minimum on card transactions.

## Returns & Refunds

If something's wrong with your order — wrong cake, decorating mistake, baking issue — please bring it back or call us within 24 hours and we'll make it right, full stop. We don't offer returns simply because you changed your mind.

## Gift Cards

We sell gift cards in any amount from $10 to $500. They never expire and can be used for anything in the store, including custom orders. Available in-store or by phone.

## Catering & Wholesale

We provide pastry catering for offices, events, and weddings. Email catering@sweetcrumb.example with the date, headcount, and your budget. We need 1 week's notice for events under 50 people, 3 weeks for larger.

We also wholesale our breads and pastries to a few cafés in town. Wholesale inquiries: ask for Maria.

## Lost & Found

If you left something behind, give us a call. We hold items for 2 weeks before donating.

## Pets

Service animals are welcome inside. Other pets need to wait outside, but we have a water bowl by the front door and dog biscuits at the counter (a single biscuit is on the house).

## Complaints

If something's wrong, please tell us in person or call (802) 555-0142 and ask for the manager on duty. We take feedback seriously and the only way we get better is when you tell us.
