# PROFESSIONAL SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Professional Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made and entered into as of June 15, 2025 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

**Northpath Marketing LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with offices at 1840 Industrial Drive, Suite 220, Wilmington, DE 19801 ("Provider")

and

**Cedar Valley Realty Group**, a Vermont corporation with offices at 88 Main Street, Burlington, VT 05401 ("Client")

(each a "Party," and collectively the "Parties").

## 1. Services

Provider agrees to provide digital marketing services to Client as follows (the "Services"):

(a) Management of Client's Google Ads account, including campaign setup, keyword research, ad copy creation, and ongoing optimization;

(b) Management of Client's Facebook and Instagram advertising, including audience research, creative development, and budget allocation;

(c) Monthly performance reports delivered no later than the 7th business day of each month;

(d) One (1) strategy call per month, not to exceed sixty (60) minutes;

(e) Such additional services as the Parties may agree in writing from time to time.

## 2. Term

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue for an initial term of twelve (12) months (the "Initial Term"). Upon expiration of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month terms (each, a "Renewal Term"), unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least ninety (90) days prior to the end of the then-current Term.

## 3. Fees and Payment

(a) **Service Fee.** Client shall pay Provider a monthly fee of Four Thousand Two Hundred Dollars ($4,200) for the Services (the "Service Fee").

(b) **Ad Spend.** Client shall be responsible for all ad spend on third-party platforms (Google, Meta, etc.). Provider may, at its discretion, place ad spend on Provider's credit card and invoice Client; in such case, Client shall reimburse Provider within seven (7) days of invoice, and a service charge of seven and one-half percent (7.5%) shall apply to all reimbursed ad spend.

(c) **Annual Increase.** The Service Fee shall increase by eight percent (8%) on each anniversary of the Effective Date.

(d) **Payment Terms.** Invoices are due upon receipt. Any amount not paid within thirty (30) days shall bear interest at one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is less. Provider may suspend Services for any account more than fifteen (15) days past due.

(e) **Setup Fee.** Client shall pay a one-time setup fee of Eight Thousand Five Hundred Dollars ($8,500), invoiced upon execution of this Agreement and due upon receipt. The setup fee is non-refundable.

## 4. Intellectual Property

(a) All deliverables, including but not limited to ad creative, written copy, audience definitions, campaign structures, account configurations, strategy documents, and reports developed by Provider in connection with the Services (collectively, "Work Product") shall remain the sole and exclusive property of Provider until Client has paid in full for the Services performed.

(b) Upon receipt of full payment for the applicable month's Services, Provider grants Client a non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable license to use the Work Product solely for Client's internal business purposes. Client shall not sublicense, sell, or otherwise distribute the Work Product.

(c) Notwithstanding anything to the contrary, Provider retains all right, title, and interest in any of Provider's pre-existing materials, methodologies, frameworks, templates, and proprietary systems used in connection with the Services.

## 5. Confidentiality

Each Party agrees to maintain the confidentiality of the other Party's Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any non-public information disclosed by one Party to the other in connection with this Agreement. Each Party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable standard of care.

## 6. Termination

(a) **Termination for Cause.** Either Party may terminate this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to the other Party in the event of a material breach by the other Party that remains uncured at the end of such notice period.

(b) **Termination for Convenience.** Client may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing one hundred eighty (180) days' written notice to Provider. In the event of termination for convenience, Client shall pay an early termination fee equal to fifty percent (50%) of the remaining Service Fees for the then-current Term.

(c) **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Client shall pay all amounts owed through the effective termination date. Provider shall, within thirty (30) days, return or destroy Client's Confidential Information, except as required for legal or regulatory purposes.

## 7. Non-Solicitation

During the Term and for twenty-four (24) months thereafter, Client shall not, directly or indirectly, solicit for employment, hire, or engage as an independent contractor any current or former employee or contractor of Provider with whom Client had contact during the Term. In the event of a breach of this provision, Client shall pay liquidated damages equal to the annual compensation of such individual.
